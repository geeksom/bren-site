The problem

Summaries are either too long to read or too short to trust.

The weekly update is written on Friday at 5pm from memory. The TL;DR channel is muted. The manager's manager asks for "a quick summary" and gets a different one from each team. Everyone is summarising; nobody is informed.

The problem is not that people don't write updates. It is that no summary is built for the person reading it.

How Bren does it

One source of truth, four altitudes.

Daily digest for team leads

What moved yesterday, who is blocked, who did not post, what needs a decision today. Delivered before the day starts, in Slack, Teams, email or the web app.

Weekly digests for managers and ICs

Managers see across their teams and dependencies. ICs see their team, the projects they depend on, and requests waiting on them. Same facts, different lens.

Weekly leadership digest and monthly recap

Momentum, risks and decisions needed across the org for senior leadership. A monthly recap for everyone — OKR progress, team contributions, wins and losses. Quarterly, half-yearly and yearly snapshots on Business and Enterprise.

Cited, permission-aware, pausable

Every item links to the message, ticket, doc or meeting behind it. You only see what you could see in the source tools. Pause digests during leave and get a catch-up edition on return.

FAQ

AI Digests: the details

Where are digests delivered?

Always in the web app, plus your choice of Slack, Microsoft Teams or email. Team digests can also post to a channel.

Can I change what goes in my digest?

Yes — follow or mute projects, teams and objectives, set the cadence, and choose sections. Org admins set defaults per role.

Which digests are on which plan?

Team: daily (leads) and weekly (managers, ICs). Business and Enterprise add the weekly leadership digest, monthly recap for everyone, quarterly / half-yearly / yearly snapshots and Year in Review.

How does Bren avoid summarising things wrongly?

Every claim is grounded in a retrieved source and linked. Missing updates are reported as missing. Any segment can be expanded to the underlying evidence, and managers can request clarification instead of assuming.
