The problem

Every "AI for work" tool asks you to trust it with your private thoughts.

Managers keep notes on 1:1s. ICs keep notes on what they really think. If those notes live in a tool that summarises everything, people stop writing them — or write something safer and less useful. Trust is the feature.

How Bren does it

Private by construction, not by policy.

Encrypted per workspace

Private notes are envelope-encrypted with a per-workspace key on top of AES-256 at rest. Workspace admins and Bren staff cannot read them. Ever.

Take them anywhere

From Ask Bren in Slack or Teams ("note to self: …"), from a timeline, or in the web app. Attach a note to a person, project or 1:1 for your own reference.

Excluded from AI by default

Notes do not feed digests, timelines, red flags or Ask Bren answers for anyone else. An organisation can choose to let people opt individual notes into summaries; that setting is visible to everyone.

Review notes, kept where they belong

Employee review observations live in the manager's private notes, attached to the person, exportable by the manager only. Bren does not produce performance scores.

Who it is for

Who writes more because of it

Managers

1:1 prep and review notes in the same place as the person's timeline — without the notes ever leaking into it.

Individual contributors

Notes to self on what to raise, what to stop doing, what you want next.

HR and works councils

A clear, enforceable line between work records and private thoughts.

FAQ

Private Notes: the details

Can a workspace admin read my private notes?

No. They are encrypted with a per-workspace key that admins do not hold, and Bren staff have no access. Deleting your account deletes your notes.

Can the organisation force notes into AI summaries?

No. The org setting only allows individuals to opt specific notes in. The default is off, and the setting is visible to everyone in the workspace.

What happens to my notes if I leave?

They are deleted with your account. They are never transferred to your manager or successor.
