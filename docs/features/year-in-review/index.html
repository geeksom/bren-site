The problem

The annual review deck takes three weeks and remembers three months.

Every December someone assembles the year from memory and whatever slides survived. The first two quarters are a blur, the losses are softened, and the people who made the wins happen are a footnote.

How Bren does it

Twelve months of evidence, one report.

Progress against objectives

Every objective and KR for the year: achieved, partially achieved, dropped — with the linked work and the moments it turned.

Team contributions

What each team owned, shipped and marked as won or lost, in their own words from their updates. Attribution without rankings.

Biggest wins and losses

From the Wins & Losses Ledger, ranked by impact on objectives, with a one-paragraph story for each and the timeline behind it.

What changed, and what it cost

Reorgs, launches, hires, meetings cancelled, hours reclaimed. Export as PDF or Google Doc; share to Slack or Teams; use it in the board deck.

FAQ

Year in Review: the details

Can I run it for any period?

Yes — calendar year, fiscal year, or any custom range. Quarterly and half-yearly snapshots are available on the same plans.

Can we edit it?

Export to Google Docs or Word and edit freely. The Bren version stays as the cited record.

Who can see it?

Org-wide reports: roles the admin designates. Team reports: the team lead and above. Everything inside respects Bren's visibility rules.
