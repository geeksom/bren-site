Monday Morning Brief · Business & Enterprise

Know where to intervene before Monday 9am.

A weekly kickoff written for you: what happened last week, what matters this week, what to block time for. Delivered before 09:00 local on your first working day — in Slack, Teams, email or the web app.

Monday Morning Brief · Elena (COO)

Last week

3 projects advanced, 1 slipped (Atlas — legal review). Support backlog down 18%.

Watch this week

Two of three Atlas owners on leave Wed–Fri. Activation KR needs 2 more launches to stay on pace.

Block time for

Q4 planning input due Thu · Board deck review · 1:1 with Priya (asked for a decision on hiring)

The problem

You found out in a meeting, from someone who had known for a week.

Leaders start Monday with a calendar, not a picture. The picture arrives piecemeal: a 1:1 at 10, a sync at 11, a Slack thread at lunch. By Wednesday you know where the fires are; by then they are fires.

A chief of staff would have handed you a one-pager at 8:45. Most people do not have a chief of staff.

How Bren does it

A one-pager, written for your altitude, every week.

Last week, honestly

Projects that advanced, projects that slipped and why, decisions made, wins and losses marked by managers — with links to the evidence.

Watch this week

Owners on leave, dependencies about to bite, OKRs off pace, red flags still open. Each with a suggested intervention you can act on before the first call.

Block time for

Requests waiting on you, deliverables due, planning inputs owed, 1:1s where someone asked for a decision. Pulled from tickets, threads and calendars.

Persona-specific by design

A VP Engineering and a COO get different briefs from the same facts. Follow or mute teams, projects and objectives to tune yours.

FAQ

Monday Morning Brief: the details

When exactly does it arrive?

Before 09:00 local time on your first working day of the week — Monday for most, configurable for other schedules and public holidays.

Is it only for executives?

It is available to anyone on Business or Enterprise; admins choose which roles receive it by default. Most companies turn it on for managers and above.

How is it different from the weekly digest?

The weekly digest looks back at what happened. The Monday Morning Brief looks forward: what to watch, what to decide, what to block time for — and it is written around your calendar.
